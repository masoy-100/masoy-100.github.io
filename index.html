Sneaker release monitor and checkout bot

2023 to 2025

Watches retailer endpoints for restocks and new drops, then runs checkout across a pool of proxied sessions before the item sells out. Built for the resale market, where inventory is fixed and speed is the whole game.

Technical decisions

  • Rotating residential proxies with per-session sticky IPs so a cart and its checkout come from the same address. Datacenter IPs got flagged within minutes.
  • Session state (cookies, CSRF tokens, cart IDs) is persisted per task so a crash mid-checkout can resume instead of restarting.
  • Monitor and checkout are separate processes talking over a queue. Polling stays cheap and steady while checkout bursts.
  • Backoff is jittered per proxy, not global, so one rate-limited IP doesn't slow the whole pool.

Decision support system for coins and precious metals

2026

Pulls live spot prices and dealer listings for gold, silver, and platinum, works out the real premium over melt on every listing, and scores each one against a buyer's goals so the output is "buy this, skip that" instead of a spreadsheet.

Technical decisions

  • Every listing is reduced to one comparable number first: landed cost per troy ounce of pure metal, including shipping and payment fees.
  • Spot price is pulled on its own schedule and cached so a 40-page dealer scrape doesn't spam the price API.
  • Scoring is a weighted model the user controls: premium over spot, dealer reputation, liquidity, numismatic upside. Change the weights and the ranking updates.
  • Historical premiums are stored so a product can be flagged as cheap relative to its own average, not just cheap today.

BTC and SOL regime model

live

A live model running in your browser. It pulls daily prices for Bitcoin and Solana, computes momentum, realized volatility, and drawdown, and classifies each asset into a regime. The point isn't to predict price; it's to know when to be sized in and when to stand aside.

How the regime is decided

  • Trend when 10-day momentum is positive and realized vol sits below its median for the window. Price is going up without thrashing.
  • Chop when momentum is near zero or vol is elevated but drawdown is shallow. Nothing to act on.
  • Stress when drawdown from the running high is deeper than 15% and vol is above median. Sizing goes to zero.
  • SOL is roughly twice as volatile as BTC, so thresholds are scaled by each asset's own vol rather than shared.
